The Veil Nebula is a supernova remnant located in the constellation Cygnus. It is the expanding remains of a massive star that exploded about 10,000 years ago.

### Target Details & Notes

Captured under Bortle 6 skies in Eugene, Oregon. 14 hours of integration was used to bring out the delicate filaments of the nebula against the light pollution.
